063 - O Come, Let Us Sing to the Lord
Words: Scottish Psalter  |  Music: Collection of Hymns, Dublin
Key: Eb  |  Time: 4/4  |  Tempo: Moderato  |  Tune: IRISH  |  Meter: 8.7.8.5
============================================================================

[Verse 1]
Cm/G    Bb/F Cm/G Eb/Bb Fm/C Eb/Bb Bb
O come, let  us   sing  to   the   Lord,
Bb   Eb/Bb Bb Cm/G  Eb/Bb
Come let   us every one
Bb       Bb7/Ab Eb      Bb7/Ab Gm
A joyful noise  make to the    Rock
Bb Fm/C Eb/Bb
Of our  salvation.

[Verse 2]
Cm/G   Bb/F   Eb/Bb Fm/C     Bb
Let us before His   presence come
Bb   Eb/Bb Bb  Fm/Ab    Eb/Bb
With glad  and thankful voice;
Bb     Eb/Bb Bb7/Ab Eb        Bb7/Ab Gm
Let us sing  psalms of praise to     Him,
Bb       Fm/C Eb/Bb  Cm/G
And make a    joyful noise.

[Verse 3]
Cm/G     Bb/F Cm/G  Eb/Bb Fm/C Eb/Bb Bb
For God, a    great God   and  great King,
Bb        Fm/Ab Cm/G Eb/Bb
Above all gods, He   is;
Bb         Eb/Bb Bb7/Ab Eb     Bb7/Ab Gm
The depths of    earth  are in His    hand,
Bb           Fm/C Eb/Bb Fm/Ab Cm/G
The strength of   hills is    His.

[Verse 4]
Cm/G   Bb/F Cm/G  Fm/C Eb/Bb
To Him the  ocean vast belongs,
Bb  Eb/Bb Bb  Fm/Ab Cm/G Eb/Bb
For He    the sea   did  make;
Bb      Eb/Bb Bb7/Ab Eb   Bb7/Ab Gm
The dry land  also   from His    hands,
Bb       Fm/C Eb/Bb Fm/Ab Cm/G
Its form at   first did   take.

[Verse 5]
Cm/G    Bb/F Cm/G Eb/Bb Fm/C    Bb
O come, bow  down and   worship Him,
Bb  Eb/Bb     Fm/Ab  Eb/Bb
And kneeling, humbly pray,
Bb      Eb/Bb Bb7/Ab Eb  Bb7/Ab Gm
Come to our   Maker  and our    God,
Bb       Fm/C Eb/Bb Fm/Ab
And hear His  voice today.
