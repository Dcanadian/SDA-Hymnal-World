521 - Depth of Mercy
Words: Charles Wesley  |  Music: William B. Bradbury
Key: F  |  Time: 4/4  |  Tempo: Moderato  |  Tune: ALETTA  |  Meter: 7s
=======================================================================

[Verse 1]
F/C   Am Dm/A       F/C   C
Depth of mercy!—can there be
F/C   Dm/A  C        F/C Gm/Bb
Mercy still reserved for me?
Gm/D   F/C     C     Bdim
Can my God His wrath forbear?
Am      Dm/A  Gm/Bb
Me, the chief of sinners, spare?

[Verse 2]
F/C Am   Dm/A C         F/C C
I   have long withstood His grace,
F/C  Am       C   Gm/Bb F/C Gm/Bb
Long provoked Him to    His face,
Gm/D      F/C     C  Bdim C
Would not hearken to His  calls,
Am          Dm/A Gm/Bb
Grieved Him by   a thousand falls.

[Verse 3]
F/C Am      C     F/C
Now incline me to repent;
F/C Am Dm/A C  Gm/Bb F/C
Let me now  my sins  lament;
Gm/D   F/C         Bdim
Now my foul revolt deplore,
Am             Gm/Bb   F/C Gm/Bb
Weep, believe, and sin no  more.

[Verse 4]
F/C   Am  Dm/A C
There for me   the Savior stands,
F/C   Am  Dm/A   C   Gm/Bb   F/C Gm/Bb
Shows His wounds and spreads His hands;
Gm/D   F/C     C     Bdim C
God is love! I know, I    feel;
Am    Dm/A   Gm/Bb     F/C Gm/Bb
Jesus weeps, and loves me  still.
